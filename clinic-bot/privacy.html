30 CLINIC BOOKING Privacy policy

Privacy policy

Effective 16 November 2026

The Clinic Booking Assistant lets patients book, confirm or cancel a clinic appointment by messaging the clinic's WhatsApp number. This policy explains what data it uses and why. It collects only what is needed to manage an appointment.

1. Who runs it

The assistant is built and maintained by Alok Mishra, the creator of Bas 30 Din, as an individual, for clinics that choose to use it. The clinic a patient books with decides how its appointments are handled. Contact: hello@bas30din.com.

2. What data it uses

  • The patient's WhatsApp phone number and WhatsApp profile name.
  • The patient name typed when booking (for example, a family member's name).
  • The appointment date and time chosen, and its status: booked, confirmed or cancelled.
  • The messages a patient sends to the assistant, so it can reply.

It does not ask for or store diagnoses, prescriptions, test results or payment card details.

3. Why it uses it

  • To show free appointment slots and book the one the patient picks.
  • To send a booking confirmation and appointment reminders on WhatsApp.
  • To let the patient confirm or cancel, and to let the clinic see who is coming.
  • To answer simple questions such as clinic timings, address or fees.

Data is never sold and never used for advertising.

4. Who can see it

Appointment details are visible to the clinic the patient booked with and to the person who maintains the assistant. Messages travel through WhatsApp, a service of Meta Platforms, under WhatsApp's privacy policy. Appointment records are stored in Google Sheets, a service of Google, under the Google Privacy Policy. No one else receives the data.

5. How long it is kept

Appointment records are kept for up to 12 months so the clinic can manage follow-up visits, then deleted. A patient can ask for earlier deletion at any time.

6. Patient choices

  • Reply CANCEL to cancel an appointment.
  • Stop messaging the number at any time. Only reminders for appointments already booked will be sent.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete booking data by emailing hello@bas30din.com or by asking the clinic directly. Requests are handled within 30 days.

7. Security

Access to appointment records is limited to the clinic and the maintainer, through password-protected accounts. No system is perfectly secure, but reasonable care is taken to protect the data.

8. This website

This page sets no cookies and contains no analytics. It is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes standard request data, such as IP addresses, to deliver web pages.

9. Changes

If the assistant changes how it handles data, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
