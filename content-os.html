Content-OS

Content-OS is a Windows desktop app that Alok Mishra, the creator of Bas 30 Din, built to organise video projects and publish a creator's own approved videos to their own YouTube channel and Instagram account.

Used by Alok and by creators he invites. No public sign-up yet.

What it does

  • Creates and organises video project folders on the PC.
  • Prepares finished videos with their title, description, caption and thumbnail.
  • Keeps a schedule. Nothing is published until the creator approves the exact video, account and time.
  • Uploads approved videos to the creator's YouTube channel with the official YouTube Data API. New uploads start private, and scheduled videos use YouTube's own scheduling.
  • Publishes approved Reels to the creator's Instagram professional account with Meta's official Instagram Graph API, optionally through the Content-OS cloud helper so they post even when the PC is off.
  • Shows upload progress, publishing history and clear errors.
  • Backs up projects to the creator's own drive.

Google and YouTube access

To upload to YouTube, Content-OS asks the account owner to sign in with Google and approve two permissions:

  • youtube.upload: upload videos and set their thumbnails.
  • youtube.readonly: read the channel's ID and name to confirm it is the right channel, and check the status of videos Content-OS uploaded.

The sign-in happens in Google's own page in the browser. The resulting access is stored only on the creator's PC, encrypted by Windows. It can be removed at any time from your Google Account permissions.

Content-OS is not made, endorsed or verified by Google, YouTube, Meta or Instagram.
