30 CONTENT-OS Data deletion

Delete your data

Effective 4 November 2026

Content-OS keeps your data on your own PC. Here is how to remove all of it, step by step. You can also ask by email and it will be done for you.

1. In the app

  • Open Content-OS, go to Settings, Accounts and press Disconnect on YouTube and on Instagram. This deletes the stored sign-ins from your PC, and for YouTube also asks Google to revoke access.
  • Stop any scheduled posts (Projects, Details, Stop). A Reel waiting in the cloud helper is stopped too, and its video and sign-in token are deleted.

2. Remove the app's access

  • Google and YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions, choose Content-OS, Remove access.
  • Facebook and Instagram: Facebook Settings, Business integrations (or Apps and websites), choose the Content-OS app (shown as Secret Knook Poster), Remove.

3. Delete the files on your PC

Run "Uninstall Content-OS", then delete the Content-OS folder in your Documents. Your projects folder (Documents, Content-OS Projects) is yours: delete it only if you also want to remove your videos.

4. Data in the cloud helper

The cloud helper deletes a Reel's video file and sign-in token as soon as the post is published, fails or is stopped, and deletes the rest of the job record (account ID, caption, time, result and post link) after 30 days. Nothing else about you is stored there.

5. Ask us to delete it

Email hello@bas30din.com with the subject "Content-OS data deletion" and your Instagram username or YouTube channel name. Any cloud helper records for your account are deleted within 7 days and you get a confirmation email. Posts already published live on YouTube or Instagram; delete them there.
